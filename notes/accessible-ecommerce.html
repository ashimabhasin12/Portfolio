NOV 2026 / FIELD NOTE

Designing shopping experiences beyond the keyboard.

Lessons from exploring voice input, conversational assistance, and practical accessibility in CartLoops.

Accessible shopping is more than adding a voice icon to a checkout page. Users need clear state, understandable feedback, and alternative ways of completing the same task.

In CartLoops, I explored speech-to-text and chatbot interaction in a Django e-commerce setting. Those features can reduce friction for some tasks, but they also raise questions about recognition errors, privacy and fallback behavior.

A strong next iteration will test the experience with keyboard-only navigation, screen readers, and realistic failure cases. The lesson is that useful software needs good defaults and thoughtful recovery paths.
