NOV 2026 / FIELD NOTE

From raw wireless signals to useful features.

A brief look at dataset structure, statistical features, and careful claims in IoT security research.

Raw I/Q signal recordings are difficult to use directly for simple diagnostic comparisons. A common first step is to organize recordings with reliable labels and metadata.

Our capstone explores segment-level statistical features such as variance, skewness, and kurtosis, with environmental context when it is available. The dataset and hardware constraints are important parts of the methodology.

A feature is not evidence of secure device identification by itself. Results need well-defined splits, repeatable evaluation, and careful reporting of limitations.
